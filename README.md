# MAOS-REVIVAL

AeroCommons' survey of old general-aviation designs, experimental and certified,
that may deserve to be revived and improved with modern manufacturing.

The targets are a 1,000 lb useful load, 1,000 nm range, and cruise above
160 KTAS (preferably above 180) at altitude, in two-, four- and six-seat classes.
The baseline engine is the Aeromomentum line.

- [docs/BRIEF.md](docs/BRIEF.md) — the brief: targets, scope, dossier contents, rules and stages.
- `docs/longlist/` — stage 1, the unranked long list.
- `docs/candidates/<name>.md` — one dossier per candidate.
- `docs/rights/` — ownership and provenance research.

Lead agent: AERO. Board record: `ORG-010` (maos-workspace).
