## The eta each brochure range needs

Our model on the original engine and standard usable fuel, from gross, no taxi, climb or descent allowance, and the brochure's own reserve. Where the brochure states a power or a speed, it is flown at that (at the most favourable of 4,000, 8,000 and 12,000 ft if no altitude is stated). Where it states neither, the most favourable altitude at the slowest cruise speed, the speed our stage 3 model flies at 55% power (a fixed eta goes furthest at the slowest speed); the last column drops that floor (any speed from 100 to 220 KTAS), which shows only that almost any brochure range is reachable flown slowly enough. Two drag readings: the design's stage 3 factor, and the cleanest type in the fleet calibration (k 1.16, the CAFE-measured Express). Above 0.85 at the stage 3 factor: **optimistic**.
