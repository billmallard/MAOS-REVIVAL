## Why it died

Cut in corporate belt-tightening after losses elsewhere; reasons 'nothing to do with the merits of the airplane' (de Piolenc 1987).

## Idea ledger (what a new airplane should keep; AERO's judgement)

- Low delta wing, engine buried in the fuselage driving a shrouded pusher fan, folding wing and fin so it fits a garage: the planform and layout Bill wants studied.
- Sparless foam-cored fibreglass stressed skin, with the wing and underbody as the main structure. Scale-up and reconstructability studies: see the dossier.

## Rights and provenance (known so far; research, not legal advice)

Rohr Inc., now a unit of Collins Aerospace (RTX). Patent US 3,931,943 expired 1993. See docs/rights (Elon, AER-3787). Owned by the stage 2 rights research (AER-3787).

## Safety record

Driveshaft coupling failure after takeoff; landed undamaged.
