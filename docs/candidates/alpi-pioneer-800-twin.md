## Why it died

Shown on Alpi stand B3-207 at AERO 2026. Planned as an Experimental kit in Europe, aiming for FAA MOSAIC (light-sport) approval in the US. Factory-built from EUR 600,000 net; kit price not published (fliegermagazin). Earlier plan (2022): 2x Rotax 912ULS/915iS, about EUR 350,000, first deliveries 2024 (aerokurier 2022). That timeline slipped.

## Idea ledger (what a new airplane should keep; AERO's judgement)

- The closest current analog of the Derringer idea (a small, fast, retractable light twin), on two Rotax 916iS: shown at AERO 2026, unflown at the show.
