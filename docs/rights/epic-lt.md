# Epic LT — rights and provenance

**Stage:** 2, rights and provenance. **Researcher:** Elon (census legwork
delegated, judgement mine). **Board issue:** AER-3787. **Date:** 2026-10-24.
**Status:** first pass, desk research from public sources only. Nobody has been
contacted. From AERO's stage 1 long list (pass; but a turbine, outside the
Aeromomentum mission, listed only to represent the 6-seat kit class).

This is research, not legal advice. It reports what public records show and
where they run out. It does not say the design may be copied.

Source quality: **P** primary or official record; **S** reputable secondary;
**L** low (wiki, forum, aggregator).

## Summary

- **No type certificate for the LT. But its certified successor, the E1000, has
  one, and it is very much alive.** TCDS A00059SE, holder Epic Aircraft, LLC,
  Bend OR; Current; Rev 7 of 12 June 2026 (FAA DRS, P).
- **The holder is an active, funded manufacturer.** Epic Aircraft, LLC and Epic
  LT Kits, LLC (both Delaware LLCs, Bend) are listed active in Oregon (P). The
  company builds and sells the E1000 GX and AX. Tanya Eves bought a majority stake
  in 2024 from the Filev family's MVF Key Investments (Wikipedia, L; TechCrunch
  author profile, S).
- **The LT design and the E1000 are one family.** The E1000 is the certified
  development of the LT (Wikipedia, L). Any LT revival would sit right next to
  Epic's only product.
- **The "EPIC LT" trademark is live, but its owner of record is "LT Builders
  Group, LLC"** (Oregon), at Epic's Bend address, renewed 2017 (USPTO TSDR, P). No
  active Oregon entity of that name was found (P). "EPIC" and "EPIC AIRCRAFT" are
  live marks of Epic Aircraft Holdings, Inc. (P).
- **The 2010 bankruptcy split the rights by territory:** the LT Builders Group
  got North American marketing rights, and CAIGA got the rest of the world
  (Wikipedia, citing Bend Bulletin, L). What happened to CAIGA's share after the
  2012 sale is not public.
- **The LT fleet is supported.** In 2022 "all but a handful" of about 50 LTs
  were airworthy and factory-supported, through Epic Flight Support (Twin &
  Turbine, S). 45 are on the FAA register (P).
- **For AeroCommons this is a benchmark, not a target.** Epic has every reason not
  to sell or license a kit version of its own product.

## Holder or seller today

- **Ownership chain** (Wikipedia, L, except where marked):
  - 2004: founded by Rick Schrameck in Bend. Chapter 11 in 2009. Schrameck
    pleaded guilty to wire fraud in 2018.
  - April 2010: CAIGA (top bidder at $4.3 million) and the LT Builders Group, a
    group of owners with unfinished aircraft, took over the company jointly. The
    builders got North America; CAIGA got the rest of the world.
  - March 2012: Vladislav Filev (S7) bought Epic through Engineering LLC; the
    Filev family then held it through MVF Key Investments (Cyprus) until 2024
    (AvWeb, "Epic Sold To Russian Firm", S, for the 2012 sale to "Engineering
    LLC"; Wikipedia, L, for Filev and the later holding).
  - 2024: Tanya Eves bought a majority share. She is described as owner and head
    of business development (TechCrunch author profile, S).
- **Oregon SOS open data (P):**
  - Epic Aircraft, LLC: Delaware LLC, Oregon registry 84607895, registered
    2012-03-30; principal place 22590 Nelson Rd, Bend. Active.
  - Epic LT Kits, LLC: Delaware LLC, registry 84348292, registered 2012-03-29;
    same address. Active.
  - "Epic LT LLC" (Oregon, 2025, Bend/Gilroy CA) looks like a private
    aircraft-owning LLC, not the maker. Not relied on.
- **LT support:** Epic split kit-owner support (Epic Flight Support) from the
  certified business (Twin & Turbine, Nov 2022, S). The last LT was completed in
  2018-2019 (L).

## Drawings, molds and tooling

- **Presumed held by Epic in Bend.** No public statement either way. The E1000
  was developed from the LT, so LT-derived data are probably part of the E1000
  design data. That is an inference.
- **CAIGA's 2010 international rights** may include data rights outside North
  America. Their status after 2012 is unknown.
- **Physical survivors:** 45 registered LTs, most airworthy.

## Kit or plans rights

- LT kits were sold only with factory builder-assist. About 50-54 were built.
  Epic stopped taking LT orders in 2012-2013 to certify the E1000 (Twin & Turbine,
  S; Wikipedia, L).
- The FAA registry kit field shows three maker names over time: Epic Air LLC, Epic
  Aircraft LLC and Epic LT Kits LLC (P). Epic LT Kits, LLC is still active (P).
- No LT kits are sold today.

## Patents and trademarks

- **Patents.** Not searched in depth. LT-era patents (filed 2004-2009) may still
  be in force until 2024-2029. The E1000 may have newer ones. **To check.**
- **"EPIC LT"**, Reg. 3211309, Class 12: live, renewed (status 21 February 2017).
  Current owner of record: LT BUILDERS GROUP, LLC, 22590 Nelson Rd, Bend
  (Oregon LLC). Original applicant: Aircraft Investor Resources, LLC (Nevada)
  (USPTO TSDR, P). No active Oregon entity named "LT Builders Group" was found in
  the state's active-business data (P).
- **"EPIC"**, Reg. 3211305, Class 12: live, Epic Aircraft Holdings, Inc. (DE),
  Bend (P).
- **"EPIC AIRCRAFT"**, Reg. 5285158, Class 12: live, Epic Aircraft Holdings, Inc.;
  Section 8 accepted December 2023 (P).
- A revival could not use "Epic".

## Fleet

- FAA registry, ReleasableAircraft database downloaded 2026-10-24: **45 Epic
  LT** records, all valid (44 amateur-built, 1 exhibition). Newest airworthiness
  dates: 2018 build (N728TH, N764LE); some re-certified in 2025-2026 (P).
- Deregistered file: 11 N-numbers matching Epic LT (P).
- 133 E1000s are registered under "Epic Aircraft LLC" (P), for context.
- **Built:** about 50-54 LTs (Twin & Turbine, S; Wikipedia, L).

## Why it died

The LT did not fail in the market. Its maker did. Founder fraud and the 2009
bankruptcy left builders owed about $15 million in parts (Wikipedia, citing Bend
Bulletin, L). The owners who took over then chose certification: the LT was
dropped in 2012-2013 so the company could spend about $200 million certifying the
E1000 (Wikipedia, L). The design lives on as a certified product. It is not an
orphan.
