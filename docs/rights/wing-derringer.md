# Wing D-1 Derringer — rights and provenance

**Stage:** 2, rights and provenance. **Researcher:** Elon (census legwork
delegated, judgement mine). **Board issue:** AER-3787. **Date:** 2026-10-24.
**Status:** first pass, desk research from public sources only. Nobody has been
contacted. From AERO's stage 1 long list (near miss: useful load -20 lb).

This is research, not legal advice. It reports what public records show and
where they run out. It does not say the design may be copied.

Source quality: **P** primary or official record; **S** reputable secondary;
**L** low (wiki, forum, aggregator).

## Summary

- **The TC has been designated "Abandoned" by the FAA.** TCDS A9WE (model D-1)
  now shows status "Historical", sub-status "Abandoned", in a document version
  dated 3 January 2023. The holder of record is still Emerald Enterprises Ltd.,
  c/o a San Diego law firm. Former holder: Wing Aircraft Company (FAA DRS, P).
- **The FAA said it would release the engineering data.** A July 2022 Federal
  Register notice says a third party asked for the D-1 data under FOIA. The FAA
  could not find Emerald, and said that if nobody answered by 3 January 2023 it
  would treat the TC as abandoned and release the requested data under 49 USC
  44704(a)(5) (Federal Register, P). Whether the release happened, and to whom,
  is not public.
- **No new aircraft can be certificated on A9WE.** The abandonment note limits
  the TC to aircraft that already had airworthiness certificates. It may be
  lifted only if the holder reappears and takes up its safety duties (P).
- **There was never a production basis.** Each aircraft needed individual FAA
  conformity inspection (TCDS, P).
- **A 1998 revival failed.** Derringer Aircraft Co. of Mojave flew a four-seat
  rework and expected a "revised type certificate". No such TC appears in DRS
  (S; P for the absence).
- **The fleet is 7 registered:** 6 type-certificated D-1s and 1 amateur-built
  D-1 (FAA registry, P).

**A mismatch in the TCDS.** Note 3 cites a notice "published ... January 3, 2023
(68 FR 47379)" about TCs "issued in the Restricted Category". The actual notice
is 87 FR 40578-79 of 7 July 2022 (FR Doc. 2022-14413), naming 3 January 2023 as
the response deadline. The D-1 is normal category. Treat the TCDS citation as a
clerical error (P for both documents).

**Absence check for a revised TC.** DRS model search "D-1" returns only A9WE
(two versions) and an unrelated 1930s ATC 256. Keyword "Derringer" returns
nothing. Positive control: model "M22" returns A6SW, as expected (P).

## Holder or seller today

- **Emerald Enterprises Ltd.** The FAA sent a registered letter on 3 November
  2021 to its last known address and tried phone and email. It got no answer
  (Federal Register, P). Its corporate status was not checked: California
  Secretary of State records were not searched.
- **Wing Aircraft Company** went bankrupt in July 1982 (Wikipedia, L;
  1000aircraftphotos, L). The registry still shows two deregistered D-1s in its
  name (N822W, s/n 4; N821T, s/n 24) (FAA registry, P).
- **Derringer Aircraft Co. LLC**, Mojave CA, showed a four-seat "Derringer GT"
  in 1998. It said a revised TC was due that year and planned one aircraft a
  week, at about $349,000 (Flight International, May 1998, S; Aviation Week,
  September 1998, S). Nothing was produced (L). Its relationship to Emerald is
  unknown.
- **Support:** none found. No manufacturer supports the type.

## Drawings, molds and tooling

- **The FAA holds some engineering data.** The 2022 notice concerns "engineering
  data in possession of the FAA". It does not say how complete that data is (P).
- **A third party has asked for it.** The requester is not named in the notice.
  If the data was released, someone outside the TC holder may hold a copy.
- **Tooling:** no public record of where Wing's tooling went. Seven unfinished
  airframes were reportedly sold after the 1982 bankruptcy (Wikipedia, L).
- **Physical survivors** exist and could be measured (see Fleet).

**Status: partly known.** Data exists at the FAA. Tooling: unknown.

## Kit or plans rights

None. The type was never sold as a kit or plans. One D-1 is registered as
amateur-built (N520SW, s/n 1000, registered May 2024, Fort Worth TX, no
airworthiness date yet) (FAA registry, P). How it was built, and from what, is
unknown. It may be one of the unfinished airframes.

## Patents and trademarks

- **Patents.** The wing used a "patented" stretch-formed, chemically milled skin
  (Plane & Pilot, S). Any patent from the 1960s expired long ago: US patents
  granted before 8 June 1978 ran 17 years from grant. The patent itself was not
  identified.
- **"DERRINGER" trademark:** not checked. The USPTO search tool could not be
  reached from this desk. Open item.

- Airworthy: unknown.

## Why it died

Hi-Shear did not put the certified design into production because of internal
problems. George Wing left in 1978 and formed Wing Aircraft Company, which built
a handful of aircraft and went bankrupt in July 1982 (Wikipedia, L;
1000aircraftphotos, L). The 1998 Mojave revival did not reach production (S/L).
A two-seat light twin had a small market, and no production basis meant every
aircraft needed individual FAA inspection (TCDS, P).

## Where an aviation IP attorney is needed
