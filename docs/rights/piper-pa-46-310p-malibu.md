# Piper PA-46-310P Malibu — rights and provenance

**Stage:** 2, rights and provenance. **Researcher:** Elon. **Board issue:** AER-3787.
**Date:** 2026-10-24. **Status:** first pass, desk research from public sources only.
Nobody has been contacted. Fed by AERO, stage 1 batch 1.

This is research, not legal advice. It reports what public records show and
where they run out. It does not say the design may be copied.

## Summary

- **A live type, still in production in evolved form.** Piper Aircraft, Inc. holds
  TCDS A25SO. The same type certificate covers today's M350, M500 and M600. A
  current-production line is the least likely case for a sale or licence.
- **Piper is owned by the government of Brunei** through Imprimis (since 2009).
- **The fleet is mid-sized:** 212 piston PA-46-310Ps on the US registry, plus 4
  turboprop conversions.
- **The practical path is modification, not revival:** used airframes improved by
  STC. This path is proven too: the JetPROP turbine conversion (Rocket Engineering)
  is an STC on this airframe.
- **A new-build copy needs counsel**, and more so here than on the P210N, because
  the type is a live product its holder still sells.

## Fleet

- FAA registry, make/model `PIPER PA-46-310P`, queried 2026-10-24: **211** piston
  aircraft under Piper's model code, plus **1** under the "New Piper Aircraft" code
  (212, matching AERO), plus **4** registered as turboprop conversions. Production:
  403 (AERO).

## Drawings and tooling

Held by Piper as proprietary type design data, and in active use for the M-Class.
There is no public record of release or licensing.

## Kit or plans rights

None.

## Patents and trademarks

- Patents from the original 1979-84 design are long expired. Piper may hold live
  patents on later M-Class changes; a Google Patents search for Piper assignments
  since 2006 was **not run**. That search matters only if we re-derive.
- "Piper" and "Malibu" are Piper marks; a revival would not use them.

## STCs on this airframe

- **JetPROP** turbine conversion (Rocket Engineering): an STC covering everything
  forward of the firewall. Some parts are available only from Rocket.

## The paths

| Path | What it would take | Assessment |
|---|---|---|
| **Buy the TC** | A transaction with Piper. | Not credible: the same TC carries Piper's current products. |
| **License** | A licence from Piper. | Same. |
| **Modify existing airframes** | Used PA-46-310Ps, improved by STC. | Proven route, with a fleet of 212. |
| **Re-derive from an airframe** | Measure one and build a new design. | Physically possible. Legally the most exposed case in this batch, because the holder sells the product. **Attorney needed.** |

## Where an aviation IP attorney is needed

1. **Re-deriving from a measured airframe** of a type its holder still sells,
   including any live patents on shared M-Class structure.
2. **Data access under an STC**, if we modify airframes.
