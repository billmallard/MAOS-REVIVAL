# Windecker AC-7 Eagle — rights and provenance

**Stage:** 2, rights and provenance. **Researcher:** Elon (census legwork
delegated, judgement mine). **Board issue:** AER-3787. **Date:** 2026-10-24.
**Status:** first pass, desk research from public sources only. Nobody has been
contacted. From AERO's stage 1 long list (near miss: speed unconditioned, range
50 nm short).

This is research, not legal advice. It reports what public records show and
where they run out. It does not say the design may be copied.

Source quality: **P** primary or official record; **S** reputable secondary;
**L** low (wiki, forum, aggregator).

## Summary

- **The TC is not orphaned.** TCDS A7SW is "Current", sub-status empty, Rev 6 of
  30 May 2017. Holder: Windecker Aircraft, Inc., 137 Knob Hill Road, Mooresville,
  NC (FAA DRS, P).
- **Every transfer since 1977 is recorded.** Windecker Research to Gerald P.
  Dietrick (1977), Composite Aircraft Corporation (1979), Theodore R. Windecker
  (2009), AC7 LLC (2013), and Windecker Aircraft, Inc. (15 November 2014) (P).
- **The owner is a Chinese investor with his own plans.** Wei Hang bought the TC
  in 2015 and holds "exclusive rights to production". He planned Chinese
  production, then a new clean-sheet design (S). No result from either was found
  after 2018.
- **The holder was still active in 2023.** Windecker Aircraft Inc. owns both
  N-registered Eagles. Both registrations were renewed, last action 14 April
  2023, expiring 31 January 2028 (FAA registry, P).
- **There is no production basis.** Each aircraft needs individual FAA
  conformity inspection (P).
- **The design patents are long dead.** Windecker held 22 US patents, most
  assigned to Dow Chemical (L/S). All were 1960s-70s grants, so all have expired.
- **The fleet is tiny:** 2 registered, both experimental; 8 or 9 built; 3 in
  museums (P/S).

## Holder or seller today

- **Windecker Aircraft, Inc.**, Mooresville, NC, is owned by Wei Hang, a Chinese
  real-estate developer. Don Atchison was CEO (AOPA 2016; AvWeb 2015; S).
- **2015:** a team led by Atchison restored serial 7 (N4198G). It flew on 6
  December 2015, the first Eagle flight in 31 years. The plan was FAA and CAAC
  validation and production in China (AvWeb; AOPA; S). The company announced a
  $6 million, 58-job investment in Mooresville (S).
- **2016-2018:** the company switched to a clean-sheet design, the "Tian Hu",
  under John Roncz. A first flight was expected in mid-2019 (AOPA 2016; Midwest
  Flyer, August 2018; S). Plane & Pilot (October 2017) described a modernised
  IO-550, glass-cockpit Eagle aimed at China (S).
- **After 2018:** no press found. The 2023 registry renewals (P) are the latest
  sign of life. The NC Secretary of State site blocked automated access, so
  corporate status was **not verified**.
- **Support:** none known for the type.

## Drawings, molds and tooling

- **Original tooling:** no source says the 1970s Midland, Texas molds survive.
  The Dietrick-era assets (1977) and the 2014-15 transfers presumably carried
  type data, since the TC moved with them. Molds are not mentioned in any
  source found. **Unknown.**
- **New tooling:** Windecker Aircraft said in 2018 it had "enough tooling to
  manufacture parts for a prototype" of the new design, not the AC-7 (S).
- **Survivors that could be measured** (FAA registry and deregistered file, P;
  NASM, P; Plane & Pilot 2021, S):
  - s/n 3, N804WR: registered, experimental R&D, Windecker Aircraft Inc.
  - s/n 7, N4198G: registered, experimental exhibition (certificate December
    2015), Windecker Aircraft Inc.; the 2015 restoration.
  - s/n 6, N4197G: Smithsonian NASM, Garber facility (gift of Dow and Tom
    Hairston).
  - s/n 4: deregistered 1997 to Lake Jackson Historical Association (TX).
  - s/n 2: deregistered 1981 to University of Texas at Austin.
  - s/n 5: reported at the Army Aviation Museum, Fort Rucker (L).
  - s/n 9 (YE-5 military variant): deregistered 1973.

## Patents and trademarks

- **Patents.** Windecker received 22 US patents on composite construction, most
  assigned to Dow Chemical (Wikipedia, L). AOPA says "Dow today owns 17 of the 22"
  (S). All date from the 1960s-70s and have expired (17-year term from grant).
- **"WINDECKER":** no US trademark filing of any kind was found (USPTO search,
  P). Positive control: the same search returns the live "MOONEY" Reg. 935574.
- **"EAGLE":** too many unrelated hits to clear in this pass. Aircraft-related
  live marks include Tronair ground-support vehicles. Not usable as a clearance.

## Fleet

- FAA releasable aircraft database, downloaded 2026-10-24: WINDECKER A/C7,
  **2 aircraft**, both experimental, both owned by Windecker Aircraft Inc.,
  Mooresville (P). No aircraft hold standard airworthiness certificates.
- **Built:** sources differ. NASM: nine (two prototypes, six civil, one YE-5).
  AvWeb and Wikipedia: eight. AOPA 2015: "serial No. 7 out of nine". Two more may
  have been built under Dietrick (L).

## Why it died

The Windeckers spent a reported $20 million on certification and ran out of money
soon after production began (AvWeb, S). Dietrick bought the assets in 1977 and
formed Composite Aircraft Corporation in 1979. He could not get the price down to
the Bonanza's: "$112,000 vs $95,000" (NASM, P). The 1980s GA collapse and
product-liability litigation finished it (NASM).
