# Extra EA-400 — rights and provenance

**Stage:** 2, rights and provenance. **Researcher:** Elon (census legwork
delegated, judgement mine). **Board issue:** AER-3787. **Date:** 2026-10-24.
**Status:** first pass, desk research from public sources only. Nobody has been
contacted. From AERO's stage 1 long list (near miss: range -89 nm).

This is research, not legal advice. It reports what public records show and
where they run out. It does not say the design may be copied.

Source quality: **P** primary or official record; **S** reputable secondary;
**L** low (wiki, forum, aggregator).

## Summary

- **The TC is live, and it changed hands a year ago.** EASA TCDS EASA.A.011
  (EA 400 and EA 400-500) Issue 07, 21 October 2025, names **Aerospace Composites
  GmbH, Laupheim** as holder. The change record reads "TC Transfer due to new TC
  holder's DOA ownership" (P).
- **The FAA record has not caught up.** FAA TCDS A43CE Rev 12 (28 July 2021),
  status "Current", still names SST Flugtechnik GmbH, Memmingerberg (P). The FAA
  validation follows the EASA TC, so this is a lag, not a second holder. Counsel
  should confirm.
- **Three insolvencies in the chain.** Extra Flugzeugbau filed in January 2003.
  The successor, Extra Flugzeugproduktions- und Vertriebs-GmbH, held the TC to
  2014. SST Flugtechnik, holder from 2014, filed on 3 February 2025 (P; S).
- **A conflicting ownership claim exists.** In November 2014 Jiangsu A-Star
  (China) said it "owns the Type Certificate and production rights" of the EA-500
  (S, Rolls-Royce release). No EASA or FAA holder record names A-Star. Who owns
  the design data, as distinct from the TC, is the key open question.
- **Small fleet:** 10 on the FAA registry (P). About 27 EA-400s were built (L).
- **No live US marks found** for EXTRA on aircraft. Walter Extra's mark was
  cancelled (P).

## Holder or seller today

- **Aerospace Composites GmbH** (Laupheim, founded 2016) makes composite
  materials and tooling and says it develops complete aircraft (L, implisense
  company profile). No press release on the TC transfer was found. The EASA
  wording suggests it bought SST's design organisation (DOA) business. That is
  an inference.
- **SST Flugtechnik GmbH** (Memmingerberg, HRB 15483) filed for insolvency on 3
  February 2025. Dr Matthias Hofmann (Pohlmann Hofmann) was appointed provisional
  administrator by the Memmingen court. SST had 7 staff and held an EASA DOA from
  2015 (aerokurier, 14 Feb 2025; fliegermagazin, 17 Feb 2025; AIN, 12 Feb 2025;
  S). Its website no longer responds (checked 2026-10-24).
- **Extra Flugzeugproduktions- und Vertriebs-GmbH** (Hünxe) is alive and builds
  aerobatic aircraft. Its website lists no EA-400 or EA-500 (P, company site).
- **Support:** the EASA TCDS still lists ICA documents (EA-05702, EA-0B702). The
  public record does not show whether the new holder supports the fleet.

## Drawings, molds and tooling

- **Unknown.** No public statement on where the EA-400 molds and tooling went.
- Jiangsu A-Star said in 2014 it had "developed production facilities in
  Zhenjiang" for the EA-500 (S). Wikipedia says Extra trained A-Star engineers
  and kept the TC (L, citing the World Directory of Light Aviation 2015-16). So
  some tooling or data may have gone to China. That is not confirmed.
- Type design data probably sits with the TC holder (now Aerospace Composites).
  The A-Star claim means the data may be owned by one party and held by another.

## Patents and trademarks

- **Patents.** No EA-400-specific patents found. A patent filed before about
  2006 would have run out by now (20 years from filing). Not searched in depth.
- **"EXTRA"** for airplanes, SN 75894212 (Walter Extra), is **cancelled** (USPTO
  trademark search, P). No live class 12 "EXTRA 400" or "EXTRA 500" mark was found.
- **Positive control:** the same search returns the live Textron "MUSTANG"
  aircraft mark, SN 76436781 (P).
- "Extra" is the founder's name and the brand of a live company. A revival should
  not use it.

## Fleet

- FAA registry, make EXTRA, model EA-400, 2026-10-24: **10 aircraft** (California
  1, Delaware 3, Florida 1, New Jersey 1, Oregon 3, Wyoming 1). Code 7021804,
  "Extra Flugzeugbau GmbH", type certificated (P). AIN counted 9 in February 2025
  (S). No EA-500 is on the US register.
- **Positive control:** the same query for MOONEY M22 returns 14 (P).
- **Built:** MSN 003 to 028 under the EASA TCDS, which fits the 27 built reported by Wikipedia (L). Six
  EA-500 serials (1002-1007) are listed (P). AIN was unsure any EA-500 was ever
  delivered (S).

## Why it died

The EA-400 needed about 16,000 labour hours to build and sold for about $1
million. Only 27 were built before Extra Flugzeugbau went insolvent in 2003
(Wikipedia, L; insolvency date P from FAA Note 9). The turboprop EA-500 never
sold in numbers. Production ended in 2015 (L). A Chinese production plan
announced in 2014 is not known to have delivered aircraft (S, 2014 release; no
later evidence found).

## The paths

| Path | What it would take | Assessment |
|---|---|---|
| **Buy the TC** | Approach Aerospace Composites GmbH (EASA holder since October 2025). | Possible, and the holder is new and small. But the A-Star claim to the design rights must be cleared first. Requires contact; not done. |
| **License** | Licence of the TC and data from Aerospace Composites, plus a release or licence from Jiangsu A-Star if its claim holds. | Same. Two parties may be needed. |
| **Modify existing airframes** | STCs on the 10 US airframes (for example, a liquid-cooled Aeromomentum swap; the TSIOL-550-C is liquid-cooled too). | Fleet too small to matter commercially. Could serve as a test bed. |
| **Re-derive from an airframe** | Measure an EA-400 and build a new design. | Physically possible. Composite layups cannot be recovered by measurement. **Attorney needed.** |

## Where an aviation IP attorney is needed

1. **Who owns the EA-400/500 design data and production rights.** EASA says
   Aerospace Composites holds the TC. A-Star said in 2014 that it owned the TC and
   production rights. Were the rights sold, licensed, or did they revert, and what
   passed through the SST insolvency?
2. **Whether the FAA A43CE holder line (SST) follows the EASA transfer
   automatically**, or needs its own transfer.
3. **Claims from the SST insolvency estate**, if the October 2025 transfer was an
   asset sale by the administrator.
4. **Re-derivation**, depending on the answers to 1 and 3.
