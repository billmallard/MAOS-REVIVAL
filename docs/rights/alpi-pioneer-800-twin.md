## Summary

- **What it is.** "Italian manufacturer Alpi Aviation will officially launch a
  new light twin aircraft, the Pioneer 800 Twin, at this April's AERO
  Friedrichshafen. First seen in 2022 at AERO, Alpi's Pioneer 800 Twin is a
  four-seat aircraft powered by two Rotax 916iS engines, each producing 160hp"
  (FLYER, 3 March 2026, S). FLYER also reports Dynon HDX avionics, a ballistic
  parachute and an AI-based pilot assistant. AERO 2026 ran 22-25 April.
- **How it compares to the Derringer, for rights purposes only.** The D-1 is a
  two-seat light twin with a TC that the FAA has designated abandoned. The
  Pioneer 800 Twin is a four-seat light twin, a live product of a live company.
  AERO owns the performance comparison.
- **Rights picture: firmly held.** Alpi Aviation is an active manufacturer, and
  this is a new product it is launching. There is nothing to buy or revive. It
  is a benchmark and a source of ideas, as Bill framed it.
- **Certification status: not established here.** FLYER does not say whether it
  will be certified, an ultralight or LSA type, or a kit. Alpi's own site
  refused automated reads (HTTP 403) on 2026-10-24.
