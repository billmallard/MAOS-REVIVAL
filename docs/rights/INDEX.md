# Rights and provenance — index

One line per candidate from the stage 1 long list that passes or nearly passes
the screen, plus the Rohr 2-175 (Bill's example) and the Cozy MKIV (the
reference). Details, sources and attorney questions are in each file. Research,
not legal advice. Nobody has been contacted. As of 2026-10-24.

**How to read "Rights picture":**
- **Open question:** the holder is unknown or unresolved, so ownership has to be
  established before anything else.
- **Small holder:** a live holder that might sell or license.
- **Firmly held:** a large or active holder; modifying existing airframes is the
  realistic path.
- **Nothing to own:** no data or airframe survives in any usable form.
