# Mooney M20TN Acclaim Type S — rights and provenance

**Stage:** 2, rights and provenance. **Researcher:** Elon (census legwork
delegated, judgement mine). **Board issue:** AER-3787. **Date:** 2026-10-24.
**Status:** first pass, desk research from public sources only. Nobody has been
contacted. From AERO's stage 1 long list (pass).

This is research, not legal advice. It reports what public records show and
where they run out. It does not say the design may be copied.

Source quality: **P** primary or official record; **S** reputable secondary;
**L** low (wiki, forum, aggregator).

## Summary

- **The M20TN has no TC of its own.** It is one model on TCDS 2A3, which covers
  the whole M20 family from the 1955 M20 to the 2017 M20V. 2A3 is "Current",
  sub-status empty, Rev 58 of 20 August 2018. The holder is Mooney International
  Corporation, Kerrville, Texas (FAA DRS, P).
- **The holder chain is clean and recorded.** 2A3 lists every transfer from 1955
  to the 11 October 2013 transfer to Mooney International (P). This is the
  opposite of the M22 case.
- **The Type S was retired before the factory closed.** The M20V Acclaim Ultra
  replaced it in 2017 (S). Mooney then stopped all production on 12 November
  2019 (S). The M20TN manual covers serials 31-0001 to 31-0143 (P).
- **Mooney still supports the type.** Its technical-publications list carries
  current M20TN manuals (P). That makes the M20TN a live product line of a live
  holder, not an orphan.
- **Buying the TC is not a realistic path.** It would mean buying Mooney's core
  type certificate for every M20 flying. A licence, or STC work on existing
  airframes, is the realistic shape.
- **Fleet:** 119 registered M20TN (P).
- **Names:** "MOONEY" is a live Mooney International mark. No US filing for
  "ACCLAIM" on aircraft was found (P).

## Holder or seller today

- **Ownership.** US Financial, LLC has owned Mooney International since 2020,
  with Jonny Pollack as CEO (Plane & Pilot, S; carried over from the M22 file).
- **Production.** Mooney shut the Kerrville line and laid off staff on 12 November
  2019. Its then owner, Soaring American Corporation, called the halt
  "temporary" (AOPA; Plane & Pilot; S). No restart has been found.
- **Website.** The support page (© 2026 Mooney International Corporation) says
  its service-centre directory was "recovered from a June 2026 website archive".
  The site now offers "contract manufacturing" (P). PACER and Texas Secretary of
  State were not searched.
- **Support for the M20TN: active.** The tech-pubs list includes MAN1008
  (M20TN, s/n 31-0001 thru 31-0143), Acclaim and Acclaim Type S POHs, and the
  master library "M20 thru M20TN" (P).

## Drawings, molds and tooling

- **Holder of record:** Mooney International, through Master Drawing List M-100
  on 2A3 (P).
- **No public statement** on the state of M20TN tooling after the 2019 shutdown
  was found. Most M20 structure is shared across models. The Kerrville plant
  still exists and Mooney advertises contract manufacturing (P), which suggests
  some tooling survives. That is an inference, not a finding.

## Kit or plans rights

None. Factory-built, type-certificated aircraft.

## Patents and trademarks

- **Patents.** No M20TN-specific patents were searched in this pass. Anything
  filed in the mid-2000s for the Acclaim would be near or past its 20-year term.
  **Open item.**
- **"MOONEY"**, Reg. 935574: live, aircraft and parts, owner Mooney International
  Corp., renewed June 2022 (USPTO TSDR, P).
- **"ACCLAIM"** in Class 12: live marks exist for tow bars (Reg. 3519903) and a
  pending RV application. **No Mooney filing for ACCLAIM was found** (USPTO
  trademark search, P). Positive control: the same search for "MOONEY" returns
  Reg. 935574. Common-law rights from use of "Acclaim" since 2006 are not
  shown by this search. **Attorney question.**

## Why it died

The Type S was not killed on its own. Mooney replaced it with the M20V Acclaim
Ultra, which had the same speed and range but a new cabin and pilot door (AIN,
2016; S). Then the whole line stopped in November 2019. Sales were poor despite
best-in-class speed, and the Chinese-owned company laid off 229 people (AOPA,
2019; S).

## The paths

| Path | What it would take | Assessment |
|---|---|---|
| **Buy the TC** | Buy 2A3 from Mooney International. | Not realistic. 2A3 is the whole M20 family and Mooney's support business. |
| **License** | A licence from Mooney International for M20TN data (M-100 drawings), perhaps with Mooney as contract manufacturer. | Plausible shape, given that Mooney is idle and advertises contract manufacturing. Requires contact; not done. |
| **Modify existing airframes** | STC an engine change onto existing M20TNs, or other M20 models. | Practical. 119 M20TN, and thousands of M20s on the same TC. STCs need the STC holder's own data, not Mooney's. |
| **Re-derive from an airframe** | Measure an M20TN and build a new design. | Physically easy. The design belongs to a live holder that still supports it. **Attorney needed.** |
