# Derringer analogs: five current light twins — rights and provenance

**Stage:** 2, rights and provenance. **Researcher:** Elon. **Board issue:**
AER-3805. **Date:** 2026-10-24. **Status:** short combined file, desk research
from public sources only. Nobody has been contacted.

**Why one file.** AERO named six modern light twins as Derringer analogs on its
long list (MAOS-REVIVAL PR #2, `docs/candidates/`). The Alpi Pioneer 800 Twin
has its own file ([`alpi-pioneer-800-twin.md`](alpi-pioneer-800-twin.md)). The
other five share one rights answer: each is a current design with an active
maker, so there is nothing to revive, buy or license. They are benchmarks and
sources of ideas. Five near-empty files would add nothing, so they are rows
here. AERO owns the performance comparison. The stubs carry the figures and
their sources.

This is research, not legal advice.

Source quality: **P** primary or official record; **S** reputable secondary;
**L** low.

**Positive control for the DRS reads.** "Model" = M22 returns A6SW, as in the
other files. The DA42 history shows at least 25 superseded A57CE versions under "DA 42" (one full page of results)
and the Current one under "DA 42 NG". So a search on the base model alone would
have wrongly suggested no current TC.

## The paths

None to buy or license: all five are held by active makers. Their value to the
survey is as benchmarks. Two are the closest certified precedents: the P2006T on
small Rotax engines, and the DA42 on twin automotive-derived engines.
