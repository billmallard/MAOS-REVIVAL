# Lancair Super ES — rights and provenance

**Stage:** 2, rights and provenance. **Researcher:** Elon (census legwork
delegated, judgement mine). **Board issue:** AER-3787. **Date:** 2026-10-24.
**Status:** first pass, desk research from public sources only. Nobody has been
contacted. From AERO's stage 1 long list (pass, speed unconditioned).

This is research, not legal advice. It reports what public records show and
where they run out. It does not say the design may be copied.

Source quality: **P** primary or official record; **S** reputable secondary;
**L** low (wiki, forum, aggregator).

## Summary

- **Same holder chain as the IV-P.** The 2017 sale named the "ES" among the
  out-of-production kits sold to Lancair International LLC (Uvalde, TX). JetEXE
  Aviation (Sacramento) bought the Lancair business from the Huffstutlers in
  October 2023 and trades as Lancair Aerospace (company release, P/S; AvWeb, S).
  **No public source lists what the 2023 purchase covered**; an earlier version
  of this file said "molds and IP", which no source supports. See
  lancair-iv-p.md for the chain and the open title question.
- **The Super ES is a variant of the ES** (the ES with the 310 hp IO-550-N), not
  a separate kit line (Wikipedia, L). The 2017 release says "ES". It does not
  name the Super ES. I read it as included; counsel should confirm.
- **No type certificate.** FAA DRS returns nothing for "ES" (P).
- **A certified cousin exists, but it is not the same aircraft.** The LC40
  (Columbia 300) was developed from the ES concept. It is on TC A00003SE, now held
  by Textron Aviation (P). Aviation Consumer reports the two share no common parts
  (S). The TC does not cover the Super ES.
- **Support continues.** Lancair Aerospace lists the ES and Super ES among
  supported legacy types (lancair.com, P/S).
- **The fleet is small:** 20 records name the Super ES, plus 80 ES/ES-P records
  that may include more Super ES builds. About 90 ES-family aircraft flew by 2011
  (P; L).
- **"LANCAIR" is a live JetEXE / Akyna Medical Management mark** (P).

## Chain of title

*Added 2026-10-25 at Bill's request (AER-3805).*

| When | Event | Source and quality |
|---|---|---|
| February 2017 | Lancair International Inc. (Redmond, Oregon, where Lancair had been since 1992) sold the older designs (including the IV, IV-P, ES and ESP; the release names "ES", not "Super ES") with "all assets, intellectual designs, and ongoing support business" to Mark and Conrad Huffstutler. They ran them as Lancair International, LLC in Uvalde, Texas, hoping to revive kit production. | Lancair release, P/S; Wikipedia "Lancair", L |
| October 2023 | JetEXE Aviation (Sacramento; Capt. Augustine Joseph) bought the business from the Huffstutlers, who "were concentrating on fleet support in recent years". Fleet support first, then new designs. | AvWeb; Kitplanes; Airframer; S |
| Today | lancair.com/about names "Lancair Aerospace KSAC Sacramento Executive Airport 6273 Freeport Blvd, Sacramento, CA 95822", CEO Capt. Augustine Joseph. Some lancair.com pages still reflect the Uvalde era (for example, a pop-up about the 2022 Uvalde school shooting), so the site is not uniformly current. | lancair.com, P/S |

Note on Wikipedia: the live "Lancair" article (read 2026-10-25) supports the
2017 sale but **does not mention JetEXE**, and its infobox still gives Uvalde
and Mark Huffstutler as CEO. It is out of date, and it is not a source for the
2023 purchase; AvWeb and Kitplanes are.

**Still open:**
- What the 2023 purchase covered: type designs, drawings, molds and tooling,
  trademarks. No public source lists it. The only asset evidence is the
  "LANCAIR" mark registered to JetEXE and Akyna Medical Management in 2025.
- Where the molds are: last firm location Uvalde, 2017.
- Whether the Huffstutlers' Texas LLC, still listed active, kept anything.

## Holder or seller today

Same as the IV-P (see lancair-iv-p.md):

- **Lancair Aerospace** (JetEXE Aviation Corporation), Sacramento Executive
  Airport, CEO Capt. Augustine Joseph. It sells Mako and Barracuda kits and
  supports older types (lancair.com, P/S).
- **Lancair International, LLC** (Texas, chartered 2015) is still listed active
  (Texas Comptroller, P). **Lancair International Inc.** (Oregon, 2003) is still
  listed active and holds the "Evolution Aircraft Company" assumed name (Oregon
  SOS, P).
- **Support:** active for the ES family, through service bulletins and parts
  (lancair.com, P/S).

## Drawings, molds and tooling

- Moved from Redmond to Uvalde in 2017 (company release, P/S). Where they went
  after JetEXE's 2023 purchase is not public; see lancair-iv-p.md.
- **Whether ES molds survive and in what state is not public.**
- Philippine Aerospace Development Corporation reportedly built 6 ES units (L).
  If so, there may have been a second tooling set or licence. Not verified.

## Kit or plans rights

- The ES kit was introduced around 2001 (some sources earlier). Kit production
  ended in 2012 (Wikipedia, L).
- No ES or Super ES kits are sold today.
- Kit rights follow the 2017 and 2023 sales. Both reports describe the assets in
  general terms.

## Patents and trademarks

- **Patents.** None identified for the ES. Anything filed before 2006 has expired.
- **"LANCAIR"**, Reg. 7828166, and **"LANCAIR AEROSPACE"**, Reg. 7828165: live,
  owned by Jetexe Aviation Corporation and Akyna Medical Management, LLC (USPTO
  TSDR, P). The older Lancair International Inc. registration was cancelled in
  2023 (P).
- **"SUPER ES":** no USPTO mark found (P). Positive control: the same search for
  "LANCAIR" returns the marks above.

## Fleet

- FAA registry, ReleasableAircraft database downloaded 2026-10-24 (P):
  - **20** records name the Super ES (19 valid, 1 pending).
  - **80** further records read as Lancair ES or ES-P. Builders often register a
    Super ES as "Lancair ES", so some of these are Super ES builds.
- **Built:** about 90 ES-family aircraft completed by December 2011 (L).

## Why it died

The ES kit ended in 2012 with the IV-P, and Lancair sold all its older lines in
2017 to concentrate on the Evolution, which itself closed later that year
(AOPA 2016, S; Wikipedia, L). Nothing suggests an ES-specific failure. It was a
company decision.

## Where an aviation IP attorney is needed

1. **Whether the Super ES variant passed in the 2017 sale** (which names "ES"),
   and then to JetEXE in 2023.
2. **Whether Textron's rights in the LC40 (TC A00003SE)** touch any ES-derived
   design work. The public record says the two share no parts, but that is not a
   legal opinion.
3. **The joint trademark ownership** and the chain of title, as for the IV-P.
4. **Re-derivation from a measured airframe.**
