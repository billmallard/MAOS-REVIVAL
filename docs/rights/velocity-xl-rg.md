# Velocity XL-RG — rights and provenance

**Stage:** 2, rights and provenance. **Researcher:** Elon (census legwork
delegated, judgement mine). **Board issue:** AER-3787. **Date:** 2026-10-24.
**Status:** first pass, desk research from public sources only. Nobody has been
contacted. From AERO's stage 1 long list (pass, speed unconditioned).

This is research, not legal advice. It reports what public records show and
where they run out. It does not say the design may be copied.

Source quality: **P** primary or official record; **S** reputable secondary;
**L** low (wiki, forum, aggregator).

## Summary

- **This is not an orphan.** Velocity, Inc. of Sebastian, Florida still sells
  XL kits, in fixed-gear and retractable (RG) forms. Its website is current
  (© 2026) and lists the XL, SE and V-Twin (P, company site).
- **The owner is the Swing family.** Duane and Scott Swing bought the assets
  from founder Danny Maher in 1992. Rocket Racing interests owned the company
  from April 2008. The Swings bought it back on 26 May 2010 (Wikipedia, L; the
  company site gives neither event). No later change of ownership was found.
- **The rights, the molds and the support are all in one live company.** That
  makes it the simplest rights picture in this batch. It also means nothing
  here is free to take.
- **The design comes from the Long-EZ, but without Rutan.** Maher scaled up a
  Long-EZ's lines. Kitplanes says Rutan "had nothing to do with the project"
  (S). No Rutan licence for the Velocity was found, and none is claimed.
- **No live VELOCITY aircraft trademark was found.** Velocity Inc.'s own 2012
  application for airplanes was abandoned. Rocket Racing's 2008 application was
  cancelled (USPTO, P).
- **The fleet is real:** 62 registrations whose model name reads as an XL RG,
  and 279 Velocity-family aircraft in all (FAA registry, P). Kitplanes (2020)
  says about 700 Velocities fly and the factory makes 12 to 15 kits a year (S).

## Holder or seller today

- **Velocity, Inc.**, Sebastian, Florida. Phone (772) 589-1860 on the website
  (P). The FAA registry shows Velocity Inc as owner of N94VA, a 1996 Velocity,
  at 200 Airport Dr W, Sebastian FL (P). That confirms the company exists as
  a registrant. It is not a corporate-status record.
- **Ownership chain:**
  - 1984: founded by Danny Maher. First flight 1985 (company site, P).
  - 1992: assets bought by Duane and Scott Swing (company site, P).
  - April 2008: bought by Rocket Racing interests to make race airframes
    (Wikipedia, L; Kitplanes "Around the Patch", S).
  - 26 May 2010: the Swings bought back full ownership (Wikipedia, L).
- **Support: live.** The website offers builder support, fast-build kits and a
  factory-assist programme. There is an owners' group (VOBA) (P).
- **Florida corporate record: not read.** Sunbiz blocked automated access
  (Cloudflare challenge). Status, officers and annual reports are unverified.

## Drawings, molds and tooling

- **Held by Velocity, Inc.**, which is still making kits from them (company
  site, P; Kitplanes 2020, S). No public statement on the tooling's condition
  or on any lien was found.
- The 1992 sale was of "company assets" (company site, P). That suggests the
  molds and drawings went with it. The 2008 and 2010 share deals would have
  kept them inside the company.

## Kit or plans rights

- **Velocity sells kits, not plans.** The builder-licence terms that come with
  a kit were not found online.
- **Rutan lineage.** The airframe is a scaled-up Long-EZ (Kitplanes 2020, S).
  No licence from Rutan Aircraft Factory is claimed by Velocity or reported by
  any source found. RAF stopped licensing anyone in June 1985 (see the Defiant
  file). Whether that matters to anyone today is a question for counsel, not a
  live issue for Velocity.

## Patents and trademarks

- **Patents.** None was identified. A proper inventor or assignee search of
  USPTO records (Swing, Velocity) was not done.
- **"VELOCITY"**, Class 12:
  - SN 85650267, Velocity Inc. (Florida corporation), filed 13 June 2012,
    "Propeller airplanes": **abandoned** (USPTO search index, P).
  - SN 77456581, Rocket Racing Composites Corporation, filed 24 April 2008,
    "Airplanes": **cancelled** (same, P).
- **Other "VELOCITY" aviation marks are live but unrelated:** Velocity FBO
  Network (Kairos Aviation, Class 37/39) and Velocity Zulu (Class 9/20) (P).
- Positive control: the same search for "MOONEY" returns live SN 72336652,
  aircraft and parts (P).
- So the name is not registered for airplanes. Velocity has used it for 40
  years, which may give it unregistered rights. **Attorney needed.**

## Fleet

- FAA registry, ReleasableAircraft database downloaded 2026-10-24 (P):
  - **62** aircraft whose model name reads as an XL with RG (for example
    "VELOCITY XL-RG", "XLRG-5", "TXL-5 RG").
  - **279** Velocity-family aircraft of all models, after removing unrelated
    makes (Aeros 912, "Turbine Legend").
  - These are builder-entered free-text names, so the counts are approximate.
  - Positive control: the same method returns 14 for the Mooney M22, which
    matches the template.
- **Built:** about 700 Velocities of all models flying (Kitplanes 2020, S).
  Wikipedia says about 700 kits sold and 500 flying (L).

## Why it died

It did not. Velocity has sold kits since 1985 and still sells them. The
company's weak period was the 2008-2010 Rocket Racing ownership, which ended
when the Swings bought it back (Wikipedia, L). The screen's open question is
performance, not survival. Its 190 KTAS figure has no altitude stated.

## The paths

| Path | What it would take | Assessment |
|---|---|---|
| **Buy** | Buy Velocity, Inc. or its XL design rights and tooling from the Swing family. | Only possible if they want to sell. Duane Swing was looking for a buyer in 2008 (Kitplanes, S). Requires contact; not done. |
| **License** | A licence from Velocity to sell an Aeromomentum-powered XL-RG variant, or a firewall-forward package for it. | Plausible. Velocity already deals with engine variants (IO-540, IO-550, turbines on the V-Twin). Requires contact. |
| **Modify existing airframes** | Fit Aeromomentum engines to new kits or to some of the 62 registered XL-RGs. Each builder is the manufacturer. | Building a bought kit is the product's normal use. Selling a package for it is a separate question. **Attorney needed** on the package, not the build. |
| **Re-derive from an airframe** | Measure an XL-RG and build a new design. | The holder is alive and still selling. Copying a live product carries the most risk in this batch. **Attorney needed;** not recommended without a licence. |

## Where an aviation IP attorney is needed

1. **Velocity's rights in the design:** copyright in drawings and manuals,
   trade dress, and unregistered rights in the VELOCITY name.
2. **What a firewall-forward or conversion package may do** without a licence
   from Velocity, and the product-liability side of selling one.
3. **Terms of any licence or purchase**, if Bill authorises an approach.
